Dr. Danushka Bandara is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Fairfield University’s School of Engineering and Computing. His research focuses on developing human-centered technologies that address real-world societal challenges.

He is a co-founder of the Digital Immigration Lab at Fairfield University, an interdisciplinary research initiative that uses computational methods to address humanitarian issues. His broader research explores trustworthy AI, human-AI interaction, computational social science, and the application of intelligent systems in healthcare and education.

Dr. Bandara is passionate about interdisciplinary research, student mentorship, and integrating emerging technologies into computer science education. His work emphasizes the responsible development and application of AI to create meaningful societal impact.

He earned his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Syracuse University.
