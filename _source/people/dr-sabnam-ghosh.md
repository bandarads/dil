Dr. Sabnam Ghosh is lecturer in Asian American Studies and American Cultural Studies at Washington University in St. Louis, Missouri where she leads the Asian American Studies minor. Her primary research is in Displacement Aesthetics in the fields of Asian American Studies, and Postcolonial Studies where *she examines how literary forms negotiate material and psychological conditions of displacement such as settlement, legality, mental health and access, to illuminate the making of intersectional, racialized, gendered, and laboring subjects*.
