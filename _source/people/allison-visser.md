Allison is a sophomore at Fairfield University in the Meditz College of Arts and Sciences, majoring in International Studies with double minors in Humanitarian Action and Economics. Her background includes hands-on work with immigrant, refugee, and displaced communities abroad. She has done research examining immigration rhetoric within the United States and internal displacement in Afghanistan. Allison has also interned for TEEEM, a global organization whose mission it is to teach students about global issues, humanitarianism, and leadership through empathy, equality, and entrepreneurship. Her primary areas of interest are international migration, forced migration, and humanitarian response.
