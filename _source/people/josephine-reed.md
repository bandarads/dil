Josephine Reed is a junior at Fairfield University studying Politics and International Studies in the Charles Meditz College of Arts and Sciences. Born and raised in Washington DC, Josephine’s primary areas of study include current governmental policy analysis, the US immigration system, and US foreign policy. Josephine currently interns at an Immigration Law firm in Fairfield, CT, and has previously interned for Maryland Congressman Jamie Raskin, serving District 8. Moving forward in her career, she begins to focus on immigration and immigration rhetoric in the media, exploring how national policy and legislation impacts the lives of immigrants in the US, as well as how the media portrays such events.
