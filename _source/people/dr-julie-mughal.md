Julie Mughal is the Senior Associate Director in Fairfield University’s Center for Social Impact and the Convenor of the Jesuit Universities Humanitarian Action Network (JUHAN). Before joining Fairfield, she worked for 20 years in the development and humanitarian fields, beginning her career at the International Organization for Migration in Geneva and at Save the Children in both the U.S. Headquarters and Pakistan. She is the author of *Land Without Hats*, a book which explores the courage and difficulties faced by widows globally as well as a number of articles on migration and on transformative pedagogies. She has most recently served as a Council on Foreign Relation’s Education Ambassador from September 2024 to May 2026. She received her B.A in political science and M.A. in International Relations from the Maxwell School at Syracuse University and her Ed.D. from Fairfield University.
