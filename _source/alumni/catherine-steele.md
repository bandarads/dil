As a Behavioral Neuroscience major with minors in Biology and Health Studies, Catherine (Cat) brought an interdisciplinary perspective to her work with DIL. Her academic interests in neuroscience, psychology, and human behavior shaped her approach to immigration rhetoric and her understanding of how public discourse influences perceptions of marginalized groups. She enjoyed strengthening her research and writing skills while exploring the neuropsychological aspects of bias and social attitudes through working with DIL. Cat has a growing interest in connecting these perspectives to how public narratives shape advocacy, policy, and responses to immigration.
